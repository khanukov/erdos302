### Small demands

Each template is a 3-uniform hypergraph on at most nine vertices. The required
cover size is two or three. For demand two, no singleton covers all listed edges;
for demand three, no two-element subset does. Smaller covers extend to such subsets,
so these finite tests prove the respective lower bounds. There are 20 templates,
994 positive injective arithmetic embeddings and 1,349 reused old configurations.
Every used edge must satisfy `b*c=a*(b+c)` with positive distinct denominators.

A human-readable example is the support `{2,3,4,5,6,12,20,30}` with edges
`{2,3,6}`, `{3,4,12}`, `{4,5,20}`, `{4,6,12}`, `{12,20,30}`. It requires three
omissions. If 4 is omitted, a second vertex cannot meet both `{2,3,6}` and
`{12,20,30}`. If 4 is not omitted but 12 is, a second vertex cannot meet both
`{2,3,6}` and `{5,20}`. If neither is omitted, 3 and 6 are forced and the third
edge still needs an omission.

### Exact weighted demands

For configurations G_i contained in R, already proved demands r_i and natural
weights w_i, require the load condition

```
sum_(i: v in G_i) w_i <= D     for each v in R.
```

For every reciprocal cover C, double counting yields

```
sum_i w_i*r_i <= sum_i w_i*|C intersect G_i| <= D*|C intersect R|.
```

An objective strictly greater than D*(k-1) therefore proves at least k omissions.
The new data has 107 multiplier vectors with 42,692 nonzero weights. Their supports
are checked to lie in the PREVIOUS retained support, not merely in the ambient block.

### No double counting and asymptotics

For fixed N put `X_N = union_q q*R(floor(N/q))`, where the union ranges over scales
coprime to Pi. Its constituent sets are disjoint. For every global cover C,

```
|C intersect X_N| >= sum_q b'(floor(N/q)),
|C outside X_N| >= size_of_the_old_outside_matching.
```

The two charges add because they concern disjoint subsets of C. If the additional
demand on `[a,b)` is g, the coprime interval count contributes

```
rho_P * g * (1/a - 1/b),    rho_P = product_(p<=71) (1-1/p).
```
